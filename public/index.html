About Me

I'm Achieng Odhiambo, a photographer rooted in the vibrant city of Kisumu, on the shores of Lake Victoria. My passion is capturing life's most authentic moments — the quiet joy, the wild celebration, the golden light over the lake. Every image I create tells a story of connection, place, and emotion.

From the bustling streets of Kisumu to the serene landscapes of the Rift Valley, I bring an artist's eye and a storyteller's heart to every frame. Whether it's a wedding, an event, or the raw beauty of nature, I'm here to preserve your memories with elegance and soul.

Wedding Photography

Your wedding day is a symphony of emotions — the nervous laughter, the tearful vows, the dance that never ends. I document it all with a fine-art approach, blending candid storytelling with timeless portraiture. Every gallery is a curated collection of your love story, delivered with care and artistry.

Event Photography

Corporate gatherings, birthday celebrations, cultural festivals — whatever the occasion, I bring professional energy and an eye for detail. I work discreetly to capture authentic interactions, key moments, and the atmosphere that makes your event unique. High-quality images delivered fast.
